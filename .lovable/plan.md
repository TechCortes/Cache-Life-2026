
# Caché Life NY — Full Website Rebuild

## Design Direction
- **Dark luxurious aesthetic** with an animated starry/space background featuring twinkling stars
- **Color palette**: Pure black background, white/cream text, subtle gold accents
- **Typography**: Elegant serif for headings (script/cursive for "What We Do" style text), clean sans-serif for body
- **Animated disco ball** element in the hero area
- **Full-width imagery** in black & white / high contrast style

## Pages to Build

### 1. Homepage (Index)
- **Starry background**: Canvas-based animated twinkling stars spanning the full page
- **Navigation**: Centered logo ("CACHÉ LIFE") with nav links on either side (What We Do, Media, What's Happening, Press) — each with hover-reveal GIF-style images
- **Hero section**: Animated disco ball GIF + "What We Do" in elegant script font
- **About section**: "ARTS & ENTERTAINMENT" heading, descriptive paragraphs about the agency
- **Partnerships / What We Offer**: Service cards — Private Event Production, Co-Produced Events, Digital Content Creation, Cultural Programming
- **Showreel section**: Embedded video player with headline "Check out our client projects"
- **Footer**: Instagram follow CTA, newsletter signup (email + join button), copyright, social links (Instagram, Soundcloud)

### 2. Media Page
- Gallery/grid of media content (photos/videos) with the same starry background

### 3. What's Happening Page
- Events listing or updates section with dark themed cards

### 4. Press Page
- Press mentions and articles displayed in an elegant layout

## Key Features
- **Animated star field**: Canvas element with randomly placed, twinkling white dots of varying sizes and brightness — runs continuously across all pages
- **Smooth scroll** and fade-in animations on content sections
- **Responsive design**: Mobile-first, elegant on all devices
- **Consistent navigation** and footer across all pages
- **Script/cursive font** for section headers matching the original aesthetic
